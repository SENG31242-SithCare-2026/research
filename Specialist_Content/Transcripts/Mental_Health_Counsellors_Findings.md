## 2. Key Findings & System Architecture Validation

### Barriers to Access
* **Primary Barriers:** Social stigma or fear of being seen at a clinic is identified as the biggest barrier preventing clients from committing to ongoing sessions.

### Telemedicine & Digital Accessibility
* **Comfort with Video Consultations:** High comfort level (4/5) for conducting full, structured therapy sessions online.
* **Network Fallback (One-tap phone call):** Deemed **Extremely critical**. Disconnections during emotional distress can be harmful.
* **Simplified "Adult Mode" for Elderly:** The most essential feature to have immediately accessible is a direct option to call the counsellor or the clinic.

### Emergency Support & Patient Safety
* **Live Location Sharing:** The feature is considered acceptable from a confidentiality standpoint, provided the user explicitly clicks to consent at that exact moment.

### AI Triage & Volunteer Support
* **AI Red Flags:** Mentions of active domestic abuse or immediate physical danger should trigger the AI to bypass scheduling and immediately route the user to a crisis helpline.
* **Volunteer First-Aid Training:** To prevent volunteers from overstepping into clinical territory, training in **Suicide risk identification and immediate escalation protocols** is mandatory.
* **Anonymous Community Consultation:** This peer-to-peer mode is considered safe and a "good tool" specifically for shared lived experiences, such as addiction recovery groups.
