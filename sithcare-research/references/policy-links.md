# Policy and Government Reference Links

## Overview

This document contains official policy documents, healthcare strategies, and government references related to mental health, digital healthcare, and healthcare accessibility in Sri Lanka.

These references support the research and system design decisions of the SithCare Mental Health Channelling Platform.

# Sri Lanka Mental Health Policy

## National Mental Health Policy 2020–2030

Description:
Official national policy document outlining Sri Lanka’s mental health strategies, healthcare objectives, service improvements, and accessibility goals.

Link:

Relevance to SithCare:
- Supports digital mental healthcare accessibility
- Highlights mental health service limitations
- Emphasizes community-based care and awareness
