# Sri Lanka Mental Health Research Notes

## Overview

Mental health has become an increasingly important healthcare concern in Sri Lanka. Many individuals experience difficulties accessing professional mental health support due to social stigma, limited awareness, financial barriers, and the shortage of mental health professionals.

Research and national healthcare policies highlight the need for improved accessibility, digital healthcare solutions, and early mental health intervention systems.

---

# Key Findings

## 1. Mental Health Treatment Gap

Many people with mental health disorders in Sri Lanka do not receive proper professional treatment.

Contributing factors include:
- Social stigma
- Lack of awareness
- Transportation difficulties
- Financial limitations
- Limited healthcare accessibility in rural areas

This creates a significant treatment gap between patients and available mental health services.

---

## 2. Shortage of Mental Health Professionals

Sri Lanka has a relatively low number of mental health professionals compared to population demand.

According to WHO mental health statistics:
- Approximately 2.5 mental health workers are available per 100,000 population.

This shortage affects:
- Appointment availability
- Waiting times
- Access to specialized care

---

## 3. Importance of Digital Mental Health Services

Sri Lanka’s Digital Health Blueprint encourages the use of digital technologies to improve healthcare accessibility and service delivery.

Digital platforms can help:
- Reduce travel barriers
- Improve appointment management
- Support remote consultations
- Increase healthcare accessibility in rural areas

---

## 4. Mental Health Stigma

Social stigma remains one of the biggest barriers to seeking mental health support in Sri Lanka.

Many individuals avoid professional help due to:
- Fear of social judgment
- Privacy concerns
- Cultural misconceptions

Anonymous and private support systems may help encourage early intervention and emotional support.

---

## 5. Telemedicine and Online Consultations

Online consultation systems can improve:
- Access to specialists
- Convenience for patients
- Time management
- Emergency support accessibility

Telemedicine became increasingly important after the COVID-19 pandemic and continues to support remote healthcare services.
