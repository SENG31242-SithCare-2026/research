# Key Observations

- Existing systems provide appointment and consultation services but lack localized mental health support for Sri Lankan users.
- Most platforms do not support Sinhala and Tamil languages.
- Emergency mental health support and anonymous community features are rarely available.
- SithCare aims to combine telemedicine, accessibility, and mental health support into a single platform.
